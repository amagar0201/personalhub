Privacy policy

Last updated: October 7, 2026

Who uses it. PersonalHub is used only by its owner's family. There are no other users and no sign-ups.

What it reads. With Google's gmail.readonly permission, it reads emails from billers and banks (for example bill amounts and due dates) in the owner's own Gmail accounts. It never sends, deletes or changes email.

Where data goes. What it reads stays on the owner's own computer. Nothing is sold, shared with others or used for advertising.

Google user data. PersonalHub's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Stopping it. Access can be removed at any time in the app or in your Google Account under Security › Third-party access.
